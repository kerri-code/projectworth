Light the Way

A movement made of light.

Project Worth began with one story, but it was never meant to stay there. Across Wisconsin, survivors, advocates, families, professionals, faith communities, schools, nonprofits, and neighbors are helping build something bigger.

One light can spark change. Many lights can guide a movement toward stronger child protection, safer systems, and a Wisconsin where children are protected sooner.
