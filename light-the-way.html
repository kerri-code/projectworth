What Project Worth is working on locally right now.

Kenosha County Child Safety, Reporting, and Prevention Survey Project Worth is gathering input from residents and professionals to better understand how Kenosha County currently recognizes, reports, responds to, and prevents child sexual abuse and grooming. The goal is to identify what is working well and where our community response can be stronger.
