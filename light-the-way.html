Project Worth Representative Commitment

Please review and acknowledge the commitment below.

Vision

To help make Wisconsin a national leader in child protection through prevention, accountability, and safer systems for children.

Mission

Project Worth works to strengthen child protection systems, advance prevention focused reform, support survivor informed advocacy, and help communities recognize risk earlier, respond sooner, and protect children before harm continues.

Project Worth Values

Prevention • Protection • Accountability • Compassion • Integrity • Courage • Grace • Hope • Higher Standards Youth Serving Organizations
