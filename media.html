Advocacy in Action

SB 333, now Wisconsin Act 200

SB 333 passed unanimously and became Wisconsin Act 200, strengthening protections related to educator sexual misconduct and student safety.

For Project Worth, Act 200 is a reminder that survivor advocacy can lead to concrete policy change.

The work continues. Act 200 is one milestone in a broader effort to help systems act sooner, communicate better, and prioritize prevention.

Wisconsin Child Protection Milestones

Legislative Progress & Policy Milestones

Policy milestones since Wisconsin Act 200. These laws are not directly connected to Kerri or Project Worth.

2025 Wisconsin Act 88 ★

Creates a Specific Criminal Offense for Grooming a Child

Signed March 6, 2026, Act 88 criminalizes grooming a child for sexual activity, adds grooming to Wisconsin’s definition of child abuse, and clarifies that consent is not a defense. View Act 88 →

2025 Wisconsin Act 89 ★

Builds on the Foundation of Wisconsin Act 200

Signed March 6, 2026, Act 89 requires school communication policies and annual training on identifying, preventing, and reporting grooming and professional boundary violations. View Act 89 →
