Data, Technology & Systems

Build better tools for the people protecting kids.

+
Tell us what you geek out about.

As Project Worth grows, we want to work with systems to identify challenges and build practical solutions. Maybe software, data, automation, AI, better workflows, or something we haven’t thought of yet. We start with the problem, then find the right technology to help solve it.

Software Data Automation AI UX Cybersecurity Cloud Databases Systems Design Hardware Process Improvement
What makes your brain light up?

No need to fit yourself into a job title. Tell us what you know, what you enjoy building, and what kinds of problems you cannot help trying to solve.

The goal: build practical tools that make people's jobs easier, strengthen the systems around children, and help protect more kids.
