Mission Statement

Project Worth exists to make Wisconsin a national leader in child protection by advancing strong legislation, strengthening accountability, and closing systemic gaps that fail to protect children and survivors.

Our work is rooted in prevention, advocacy, awareness, survivor-informed reform, and systems change.

✦

Programs & Services

Project Worth provides advocacy, education, public awareness, survivor-informed systems-change work, and community engagement focused on strengthening child protection in Wisconsin and beyond.

1

Child Protection Advocacy

We advocate for stronger laws, clearer reporting systems, and more accountable child-serving institutions.

2

Prevention & Awareness

We help communities recognize risk earlier, respond sooner, and build safer environments for children.

3

Survivor-Informed Reform

We use lived experience, professional insight, research, and community partnership to help inform meaningful policy change.
