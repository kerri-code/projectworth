Knowledge assessment

Demonstrate what you know.

20 original scenario-based questions. Pass with at least 18/20 (90%, equivalent to 9/10) and answer every critical safety question correctly. Up to three attempts; records require a connected portal service.

Facilitator readiness

Teach it in your own voice.

Prepare a 5–8 minute segment and one scenario for review with Kerri or Zac. A meeting request is not a confirmed appointment.

Your demonstration

Explain. Apply. Respond.

Explain all seven module messages, retell Run to the Roar, give a brief 30-second introduction, and demonstrate one respectful redirect.

Facilitation standards

Keep the room safe.

Protect privacy, avoid investigating or collecting trauma details, honor reporting duties, and model Integrity, Dignity, Courage, Collaboration, Stewardship, and Hope.

Before submission · Required agreement

Carry the light with integrity.

This facilitator agreement builds on your existing Project Worth representative commitment and is required before a teach-back scheduling confirmation can be submitted.

Respect the materials. Use Project Worth slides, guides, workbooks, and forms only for approved training. Please get written permission before copying, forwarding, posting, adapting, or sharing them beyond your authorization. This protects the message we carry together.

Light the Way Facilitator Agreement

I agree to:

  1. Represent Project Worth professionally. Uphold Integrity, Dignity, Courage, Collaboration, Stewardship, and Hope. Engage with people and systems respectfully and work collaboratively, not combatively.
  2. Protect curriculum integrity. Use approved training materials and reporting guidance, credit Project Worth, and seek written permission before changing required content or extending authorization to others.
  3. Maintain safe learning boundaries. Never solicit personal disclosures or identifying case details. Respect participants' choices, privacy, and dignity; facilitate education rather than therapy, investigation, or legal advice.
  4. Respond responsibly. Take immediate safety concerns seriously, follow applicable external reporting duties without delay, never promise secrecy, and document only appropriate factual information through approved processes.
  5. Support ethical research and outreach. Share a relevant, approved Project Worth research opportunity with each host organization using official channels. Participation is voluntary; do not collect private responses or case information.
  6. Honor authorization and accountability. Teach only after written approval and within the approved scope. Approval may be paused or withdrawn. Completing this course does not make me an employee, spokesperson, investigator, or accredited continuing-education provider.
