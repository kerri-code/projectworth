Who We Are

About Project Worth

Protecting Kids. Changing Systems. Empowering Survivors.

✦
Our Vision

For Wisconsin’s children to be the most protected in the nation.

A future where every child is safeguarded by systems that are proactive, connected, and accountable, setting the national standard for safety, prevention, and response.

★
Our Mission

Make Wisconsin a national leader in child protection.

Project Worth works to close the gaps that fail children and survivors by advancing stronger laws, accountability, and prevention-driven reform across Wisconsin.

★
★ 100% Volunteer-Led

Project Worth is fully volunteer-led. Every resource entrusted to us is handled with care, integrity, and commitment to our mission.

Our Values

The principles behind every action we take.

★

Protection First

Children deserve systems that act early, consistently, and decisively to keep them safe.

♡

Survivor-Informed Reform

Lived experience helps shape stronger systems and better protection.

⚖

Higher Standards & Accountability

Child-serving institutions must uphold standards of safety, judgment, transparency, and accountability, not simply the absence of a criminal record.

★

Nonpartisan

Child protection is not political. We work across perspectives to advance solutions that keep children safe.

🧭

Courage to Speak

Brave voices, honest conversations, and survivor-centered reform can build safer systems for the next generation.

📚

Research & Evidence

Strong reform should be guided by research, data, survivor experience, and real-world system gaps.

★ ★ ★ ★ ★

★ We are building toward a Wisconsin where prevention leads, accountability is expected, and children always come first.
